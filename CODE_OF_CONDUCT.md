# BreakFree — Code of Conduct

BreakFree should be a place where people can create, ask questions and learn without harassment.

## Everyone belongs

We welcome contributors and users regardless of experience, nationality, ethnicity, disability, age, religion, gender identity or sexual orientation. LGBTQ+ people are explicitly welcome.

Be considerate. Discuss the work rather than attacking the person. Give useful feedback, respect boundaries, credit other people's contributions and make room for beginners.

Harassment, threats, slurs, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, unwanted contact and publication of private information are not acceptable. Neither are repeated disruption or attempts to drive people out because of who they are.

## Scope and moderation

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests and other spaces explicitly maintained for BreakFree. Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation when needed. Responses should be proportionate and consider context and repeated behavior.

For non-sensitive moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through an appropriate existing project thread without repeating abusive material. For sensitive or urgent situations, use GitHub's reporting tools rather than posting private details publicly. A dedicated private project contact has not yet been established.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of affiliation with an external community program.
